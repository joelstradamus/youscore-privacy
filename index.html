You Score Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

You Score respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the You Score mobile application and related services.

1. Information You Provide

Depending on how you use You Score, information you provide may include:

  • Email address and account information.
  • Team, league, player, coach, and roster information.
  • Baseball or softball game information.
  • Scores, statistics, plays, pitches, substitutions, and other game events.
  • Photos or other content that you choose to upload.
  • Subscription or entitlement information associated with your account.
  • Information you send when contacting support.

2. Account and Authentication Information

You Score may use third-party infrastructure, including Supabase, to provide account authentication, database services, synchronization, storage, and other application functionality.

Authentication information is used to identify your account, protect access to your data, and provide features that require you to be signed in.

3. Game, Team, and Player Information

You Score is designed to record and display baseball and softball information. Information entered into the application may include player names, team names, rosters, game results, statistics, scoring events, and related information.

Some game, team, scoreboard, or player information may be visible to other users or members of the public when a feature is configured for public viewing. Users should not enter or publish information that they are not authorized to share.

If information about a minor is entered into You Score, the person entering or publishing that information is responsible for having appropriate authority or permission to do so.

4. Photos and User Content

If You Score allows you to upload photos or other user content, that content may be stored to provide the feature you selected. Content associated with publicly available teams, games, or profiles may also be visible to other users.

5. Subscriptions and Payments

Purchases and subscriptions made through the Android application are processed by Google Play. You Score does not receive your complete payment card number.

You Score may receive information from Google Play that is necessary to verify a purchase or subscription and determine whether paid features should be available to your account.

6. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain You Score.
  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Save and synchronize games, teams, players, and statistics.
  • Provide live or shared scoreboard functionality.
  • Provide subscription features.
  • Protect accounts and prevent misuse.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve application reliability.
  • Respond to support, privacy, or account requests.

7. Service Providers

You Score may use third-party service providers to operate portions of the application. These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services.

Current services may include:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, storage, synchronization, and backend services.
  • Google Play — application distribution, purchases, subscriptions, and purchase verification.

These providers maintain their own privacy practices and policies.

8. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information handled by You Score. However, no internet-connected service or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

Information may be retained while your account is active and as necessary to provide You Score's services.

When an account is deleted, associated personal data will be deleted or anonymized unless retention is reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, dispute resolution, financial recordkeeping, or other legitimate purposes.

10. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your You Score account and associated personal data.

To request account deletion, email: youscore.app@gmail.com

Please identify the email address associated with your You Score account. We may need to verify that you are the account owner before completing the deletion request.

Deleting a You Score account does not necessarily cancel an active Google Play subscription. Google Play subscriptions can be managed separately through your Google Play account.

11. Your Choices

You may contact us to ask questions about information associated with your account, request correction of inaccurate personal information, or request deletion where applicable.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as You Score changes. When material changes are made, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated.
